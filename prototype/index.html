Where the points come from

Weighted points each criterion adds to an option's total. The bold figure is the best in its row.

How to read the label: Robust wins 85% or more of runs, Likely 65–85%, Fragile 50–65%, and Coin flip under 50% or a tie. A fragile result is a sign to firm up your scores on the criteria at right before deciding.

Mark how sure you are about each criterion's scores. The figure is how often doubt about that criterion alone hands the lead to another option.

The weight that flips the result

Each row moves one weight while the others keep their proportions. “Holds” means no setting of that weight changes the leader.
